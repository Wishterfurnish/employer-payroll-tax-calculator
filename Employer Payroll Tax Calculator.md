## 🔍 Calculation Methods

| **Method** | **Description** | **Best For** |
|-------------|-----------------|--------------|
| **FICA** | 6.2% + 1.45% | Employer share |
| **FUTA** | 6.0% on first $7k | Federal |
| **SUTA** | State rate | State |
| **Pre-tax** | 401k, HSA | Reduces taxable |
| **Post-tax** | Roth, garnishment | Net only |
| **YTD** | Running totals | Reporting |
